Important Internet Safety Tips

1. Think Before Clicking

Check suspicious links before opening them. Visit important websites by typing their official address yourself.

2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Use an additional sign-in verification method wherever possible.

3. Update Your Devices

Install software and security updates to fix known vulnerabilities.

4. Use Secure Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive financial activities on public Wi-Fi. Never ignore browser security warnings.

5. Verify Online Offers

Be suspicious of unexpected prizes, urgent payment requests, and offers that seem too good to be true.

6. Report Suspicious Activity

Keep relevant evidence and report suspected fraud or abuse to the appropriate authorities.

Cybercrime Help in India

What should you do if you face online fraud?

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money has been lost.
  2. Preserve transaction details, messages, screenshots, and other relevant evidence.
  3. Report suspected cybercrime through the official Indian cybercrime reporting portal.
  4. For financial cyber fraud, call the national helpline 1930 as soon as possible.
  5. Never share your OTP or pay someone who promises to recover your money for a fee.

Official portal: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

Frequently Asked Questions

What is internet safety?

Internet safety means using online services carefully to protect your personal information, accounts, devices, and wellbeing.

What is phishing?

Phishing is an attempt to trick people into revealing sensitive information or opening harmful links by pretending to be trustworthy.

Should I share my OTP with a caller?

No. Never disclose your OTP, PIN, or password to callers or people messaging you.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious link?

Do not click it. Verify the sender independently and report or delete the message if appropriate.
